# Outreach copy contract

Use the configured target language. This contract applies only after eligibility
on the form page has passed. Do not draft for ineligible recipients.

Choose one or two verified recipient facts and one relevant proposal supported
by approved_facts. Explain why this company is being contacted. Mention only
capabilities that support that proposal; do not list every product feature.
If a useful, evidence-backed improvement can be offered briefly, put it before
the request for a conversation. Never invent a diagnosis, unobserved ranking,
estimated loss, personal experience, praise or recipient problem. A review count
alone does not prove poor operations or lost revenue. If relevance cannot be
supported, hold that row rather than manufacture a reason to contact it.

Request one next action. A meeting is optional, not the default requirement.
Only offer existing materials, verified trials, prices, discounts, examples and
service scope. Use respectful natural prose, a brief introduction and concrete
language; remove exaggerated praise, excessive apologies and fear-based claims.
For Japanese, aim initially for 300–500 characters only when useful; actual form
limits take precedence. Never pad copy to meet a target length.

Map each product claim to approved_facts and each recipient claim to dated source
evidence in facts_json/evidence_json. Keep hypotheses explicit and omit them if
they add no useful proposal. Stop research once eligibility, relevant recipient
facts and supporting product facts are available. Do not browse for decoration.

Keep uncertainties private. Do not advance copy with [TODO], ［要確認］, unfilled
placeholders or unresolved required facts to pending_approval. Hold only that row
and continue independent candidates. Check facts, one clear next action, natural
language and field length limits. Save the exact subject (if present), message,
field values and bindings, read back, then hash with payload. Outreach inputs
those approved strings without improvising or rewriting them.
